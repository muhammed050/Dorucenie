# BUILD DORUČENIE — PRODUCTION-READY COURIER PERFORMANCE & DELIVERY SLA MONITOR

You are the lead architect, senior full-stack engineer, database engineer, security engineer, DevOps engineer, and QA engineer responsible for building the entire Dorucenie SaaS product.

You are not writing a prototype.

You are building a production-ready SaaS that can be deployed publicly and used by real paying customers.

The product domain is:

https://dorucenie.com

The product name is:

Doručenie

Core positioning:

> Know which couriers are hurting your business before your customers complain.

Doručenie is a B2B SaaS for e-commerce businesses that monitors courier performance, delivery SLAs, shipment delays, failed deliveries, and courier reliability.

It is NOT a courier company.

It is NOT a route optimization platform.

It is NOT a driver management system.

It is NOT another generic order tracking application.

Its primary value is:

> Doručenie analyzes delivery data and tells merchants which couriers, countries, services, and shipments are causing SLA failures and customer-impacting delays.

==================================================
18. JOB HEARTBEAT
=================

Long-running jobs must renew their lease.

Heartbeat approximately every 60 seconds.

Update:

locked_until = now() + 15 minutes

Only if:

job status = processing

and job is still owned by the worker.

Create a worker ownership token if needed.

Do not allow Worker B to extend Worker A's job.

==================================================
19. WORKER
==========

Worker processes small jobs.

One job should normally represent one shipment check or another small atomic unit.

Default batch:

20 jobs.

Do not create a worker that processes thousands of shipments in one invocation.

Worker lifecycle:

claim
→ process
→ success
→ completed

or:

claim
→ error
→ retry scheduling

or:

claim
→ max attempts
→ failed

22. WORKER FAN-OUT
==================
